# Bosmon

Bosmon is an original monster-catching RPG that you play right in the browser. It is for anyone who loves the feeling of wandering tall grass looking for new creatures, on a computer or a phone.

---

## What it does

- Explore Solara, a connected region of 10 towns and 15 routes
- Walk through dark grass to run into wild monsters
- Battle monsters to weaken them, then throw capture orbs to catch them
- Earn experience from battles and catches to level up your companions
- Choose which companion leads your team
- Heal your team by talking to town keepers

## Monsters to discover

- Eight original species, including Spriglet, Cindertail, Bubblot, Zippip, Shroomshade, Pebblisk, Frostflutter, and Bloomfawn
- A field guide that fills in as you catch each species
- A collection quest with a research reward in the journal

## Your adventure

- A bag of capture orbs and potions, plus a shop to restock them
- A world map that tracks which towns and routes you have explored
- Progress saves automatically in the browser
- Play instantly as a guest, no account needed
- Sign in with email or Google to keep your adventure across devices

## Explore together

- See other signed-in explorers walking around the map
- Browse a list of fellow explorers with their species count and highest level

## Play anywhere

- Full-screen world with no page scrolling
- Move with the keyboard, the mouse, or an on-screen D-pad
- Simple two-button controls for talking, confirming, and opening the menu
- Phone layouts for both portrait and landscape
